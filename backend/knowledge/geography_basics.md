# World Geography

## Continents and Oceans

Earth has seven continents: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Australia, Europe, North
America, and South America. It has five oceans: the Pacific, Atlantic, Indian,
Southern, and Arctic. The Pacific Ocean is the largest ocean on Earth.

## Rivers and Mountains

The Nile is one of the longest rivers in the world and flows through
northeastern Africa. Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level,
located in the Himalayas on the border of Nepal and China.
