# Introduction to Biology

## Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy. It occurs in the
chloroplasts of plant cells, where the pigment chlorophyll absorbs light. The
process takes in carbon dioxide and water and produces glucose and oxygen.

## Cellular Respiration

Cellular respiration releases the energy stored in glucose. In eukaryotic cells,
most of this energy is produced in the mitochondria. The process consumes oxygen
and releases carbon dioxide and water.

## The Cell

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of all living organisms.
Cells are broadly divided into prokaryotic cells, which lack a nucleus, and
eukaryotic cells, which contain a membrane-bound nucleus.
