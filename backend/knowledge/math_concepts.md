# Mathematics Concepts

## The Pythagorean Theorem

The Pythagorean theorem states that in a right triangle, the square of the
length of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides.
It is written as a squared plus b squared equals c squared.

## Prime Numbers

A prime number is a whole number greater than 1 that has no positive divisors
other than 1 and itself. The first prime numbers are 2, 3, 5, 7, and 11.

## Circles

The area of a circle equals pi times the radius squared. The circumference of a
circle equals two times pi times the radius.
