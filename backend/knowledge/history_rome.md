# A Short History of Rome

## The Roman Republic

The Roman Republic was established in 509 BC after the overthrow of the Roman
monarchy. Power was held by elected magistrates and the Senate. The Republic
lasted until the rise of the Roman Empire.

## Julius Caesar

Julius Caesar was a Roman general and statesman. He was assassinated in 44 BC on
the Ides of March. His death led to a series of civil wars that ended the
Republic.

## The Roman Empire

The Roman Empire began in 27 BC when Augustus became the first Roman emperor.
The empire reached its greatest territorial extent under the emperor Trajan in
the early second century AD.
